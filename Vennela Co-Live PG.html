Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before booking? Here are answers to what prospective residents ask most.

What meals are included in the monthly rent? +
All 3 meals (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner) along with evening hot tea/coffee are included in your monthly rent. On weekends, special lunch and dinners are prepared. We maintain separate hygienic preparation standards.
What is the security deposit and lock-in period? +
We require only 1 month of refundable security deposit. There is a nominal 3-month lock-in period, followed by a simple 30-day notice period if you ever decide to move out.
Are there any curfew restrictions for working professionals? +
We understand the modern tech and medical schedule. Residents have personalized biometric smart access. If you are returning late from your shift or airport, you can enter safely using your authorized scan.
How fast and reliable is the internet connection? +
We have dedicated dual-line fiber connections with speeds up to 300 Mbps, supported by commercial mesh routers on every floor. The generator ensures zero Wi-Fi drop during power failures.
